Business Verification Privacy Notice

Version 1.1 · Effective 7 August 2026

This notice applies specifically to Kaarox business verification and supplements the Kaarox Privacy Policy. Business verification is optional unless Kaarox identifies a safety or legal reason that requires it. An account that does not complete verification will not receive a business-verification badge.

1. Who is responsible

Kaarox is responsible for the business-verification information it controls. Questions and privacy-rights requests may be sent to support@kaarox.com.

2. Information Kaarox collects

  • Kaarox account identifier and signed-in email.
  • Country, legal business name, trading name, registration or licence number, issuing authority, any applicable expiry date, registered activities, optional tax/VAT identifier and website.
  • The representative's name, stated role and confirmation of authority.
  • A current business licence or registration document and, when required, evidence of the representative's authority.
  • Verification decisions, registry-check references, dates, audit records, appeals and fraud-prevention signals.

3. Identity and biometric processing by Veriff

Veriff conducts the representative identity check. Depending on the configured verification flow, Veriff may collect an identity document, facial image, video or liveness information and may perform biometric facial comparison and fraud checks. Kaarox receives the identity-verification result, session reference and limited supporting information needed to administer the verification.

The Kaarox business-evidence form does not separately upload the representative's identity document or selfie to Kaarox storage. Veriff's own privacy information and the Kaarox–Veriff agreement govern Veriff's retention and processing of information handled within Veriff's service.

4. Purposes

  • Confirm that the representative is a real person.
  • Check that the business registration or licence is active and matches the business profile.
  • Confirm that the representative is authorised to act for the business.
  • Issue, refuse, suspend, expire or revoke the verification badge.
  • Prevent impersonation, forged documents, fraud and misuse of Kaarox.
  • Handle appeals, reports, security incidents and legal obligations.

5. Who receives the information

Information may be processed by Kaarox personnel or authorised reviewers, Veriff, Kaarox's cloud and security providers, professional advisers, and official business registries or issuing authorities in the selected country when needed to verify the submitted registration. Information may also be disclosed to competent authorities when required by law. Access is limited according to role. Raw evidence is not displayed publicly.

6. International processing

Kaarox's service providers, reviewers and the relevant official registry may process information outside the user's country and outside the United Arab Emirates. Kaarox will use applicable contractual, security and organisational safeguards for such processing and will document relevant provider locations and subprocessors.

7. Retention

Business documents and authority evidence are scheduled for deletion 90 days after the final verification decision or completion of an appeal, whichever is later, unless longer retention is required for an active investigation or by law. Verification outcomes and limited audit records may be retained for the life of the badge and for up to 24 months afterward for fraud prevention, disputes and audit purposes.

Veriff's retention must be configured separately under the Kaarox–Veriff service settings and agreement. Kaarox should select the shortest retention compatible with verification, fraud prevention and legal requirements.

8. Security

Kaarox uses restricted access, encryption in transit, backend-only approval, audit records and access logging. Uploaded business evidence is stored under non-public paths and is made available to authorised reviewers only through short-lived access links.

9. Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of eligible information, restrict or object to eligible processing, withdraw consent where consent is relied upon, or challenge an automated result and request human review. Withdrawal during verification will stop the verification and no badge will be issued. Some information may be retained when required for legal claims, security or compliance.

10. What the badge does not mean

The badge means that Kaarox completed the checks described on the badge details as of a stated date. It is not government certification, a recommendation, or a guarantee of service quality, financial standing, regulatory compliance or future conduct.

11. Changes

Kaarox may update this notice when the verification process, service providers or applicable requirements change. A new version date will be shown, and new consent will be requested when a material change requires it.
