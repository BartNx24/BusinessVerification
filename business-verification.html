Business details

Select the country where the business is registered.

Business registration evidence

PDF, JPEG, PNG or WebP. Maximum 5 MB. Kaarox validates the actual file type and file hash on the server.

Representative

Required for an authorized representative or when authority is established by a separate document.
After the business evidence is submitted, Kaarox opens Veriff for the representative's government-ID and face/liveness identity check. Kaarox does not ask you to upload the ID or selfie into this business form.

Confirmations

A badge is not issued until the Veriff identity decision is approved and Kaarox approves the business registration and representative authority.
